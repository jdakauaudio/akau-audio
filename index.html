Who am I?

My name is James Dean.  I am a composer, sound designer, musician and general audio geek.  With over eight years experience working in audio post production and twenty years playing and writing music, sailing the new waters of interactive audio is an adventure I couldn't resist.
I have worked on games ranging from low spec mobile devices to high end VR, addressing all kinds of technical challenges without compromising audio quality.  As of 2019 I have been working full time in Game Audio for a company I formed, Ākau Audio. 
 
I love tackling challenges and genuinely enjoy using technology to find new creative ways to tell compelling stories with sound.
